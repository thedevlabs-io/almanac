Five ways this could look

Same fake data in all five, covering everything Almanac tracks. Each one is a single page standing in for both the dashboard and the report. Bottom-right button flips light and dark.

1
Ledger

Quiet and typographic. No progress bars at all: numbers live in monospace rows, sections are divided by rules rather than boxes. The year heatmap is the only graphic.

Dense twelve-column grid, everything on one screen, tables instead of bars. Adds an hour-of-day sparkline and a weekday-by-hour heatmap. Most information per pixel, least hand-holding.

Hybrid · Focus + Console

Console's density and type, Focus's navigation. A six-figure stat strip that stays put on every tab, then five pill tabs over a compact twelve-column grid of tables. Nothing is crowded because only one tab is on screen, and nothing is hidden because the headline numbers never move.
